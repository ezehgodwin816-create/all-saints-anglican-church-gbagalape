About Our Church

A warm Anglican family dwelling together in unity

Who We Are

All Saints Anglican Church Gbagalape Phase I is a parish of the Keffi-Karshi Missionary Diocese within the Anglican Communion. We are located in Nyanya Archdeaconry and exist to worship God, proclaim the Gospel of Jesus Christ, and build a loving community where every member finds a spiritual home.

Our 2026 theme is taken from Psalm 133:1 – “Behold, how good and pleasant it is when brothers dwell together in unity!” We desire to be a church where unity, love, openness and mutual care are lived out daily.

Our Anglican Identity

We are Christians by grace and Anglicans by choice. We hold to the historic faith once delivered to the saints, centred on Scripture, the Creeds, the Sacraments of Baptism and Holy Communion, and the historic episcopate. Our worship follows the rich liturgical tradition of the Anglican Church while remaining warm, joyful and accessible to all.
