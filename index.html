You are welcome here

Whether you are new to the area, returning to faith, or looking for a spiritual home, All Saints Gbagalape is a warm Anglican family under the Keffi-Karshi Missionary Diocese (Anglican Communion).

We gather every Sunday for Holy Communion, praise, teaching from God’s Word, and fellowship. Come as you are — you will find a place to belong.
