Worship with Us

Come and experience the presence of God in Word and Sacrament

Sunday Holy Communion

Every Sunday · 8:00 AM Prompt

We gather for a full Anglican Holy Communion service with hymns, Scripture readings, sermon, prayers and the celebration of the Lord’s Supper. Everyone is welcome.

Weekly Programme

  • Monday – Bible Study · 6:00 – 7:00 PM
  • Tuesday – Women Fellowship · 6:00 PM
  • Wednesday – Midweek Encounter · 6:00 – 7:00 PM
  • Thursday – EFAC Fellowship · 6:00 PM
  • Friday – Anglican Youth Fellowship (AYF) · 6:00 PM
  • 2nd Saturday – PCC Meeting · 6:00 PM
  • Sunday – Confirmation Class · 4:00 PM

What to Expect on Sunday

Whether you are new to Anglican worship or returning after a long time, you will find a friendly congregation ready to welcome you. Dress is modest and comfortable. Children are welcome and there is space for families. Holy Communion is open to all baptised Christians who wish to receive.

Our current liturgical colour is Green (Ordinary Time / Trinity season).
